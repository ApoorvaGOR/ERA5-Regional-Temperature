# Data, figure, and map licensing

The ERA5-derived workbook, monthly and annual tables, summaries, figures, and
data embedded in explorer.html are distributed under **Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. This does not transfer ownership
of the underlying Copernicus/ECMWF data to the repository contributors.

License and full legal terms: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en

Give appropriate credit, link the license, and identify changes. Suggested attribution:

> Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) / ECMWF ERA5 data.
> Modifications include regional selection, Kelvin-to-Celsius conversion,
> monthly and annual averaging, spatial summaries, and visualization.
> Distributed under CC BY 4.0.

The official CDS ERA5 catalogue displays the CC-BY license. The original
download request and license acceptance record were not supplied. See
docs/PROVENANCE.md for the distinction between verified file evidence and
source catalogue references.

Natural Earth coastline and country-boundary data in data/boundaries are
public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ .
Map outlines are made with Natural Earth, at 1:110 million scale. They are
context layers and do not determine which grid points enter any average.
