ERA5 regional temperature explorer

Explore 40,401 locations across latitude, longitude, and time. This is 2 m air temperature over the supplied regional rectangle, including land and ocean. Source sampling is once daily at 17:00 UTC; annual values average the 12 monthly sample means.

Spatial pattern

Click the map to select a grid point. Axes show degrees east and north.

Annual temperature at a location

Blue: selected grid point. Orange: latitude-weighted domain mean. Vertical marker: selected year. Annual means give equal weight to each of the 12 months.

Data: ECMWF / Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), modified by regional selection, conversion to °C, and aggregation. CC BY 4.0. No station observations or land mask are implied. Trends describe this record and are not forecasts. All values are embedded: this page works offline. Embedded annual location values are rounded to 0.01 °C; use repository tables for analysis. Outlines: Natural Earth (public domain). See README and methodology.
